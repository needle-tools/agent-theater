### Fonts fetched at runtime

The text tool offers about a dozen display faces that are loaded lazily from
Google Fonts (`src/lib/collage/webfonts.ts`). They are OFL faces and are not
redistributed by this repository — but they are a request to a third party from
the reader's browser, which is worth knowing given that the README's pitch is
that a page's own tools keep the user's data out of third-party services. Only
the font files travel; nothing about the document does.

## Audio

`static/audio/` — music beds, stings and effects, generated by Needle rather
than sourced. **Needs confirmation:** whether the generator's terms permit
redistribution under this repository's MIT licence. Most do for commercial use;
some reserve rights on the output or require attribution, and which one applies
is a question about the account that generated them, not about the files.

`static/audio/manifest.json` describes what each sound is but records no
provenance. Worth adding a `source` field to each entry once the above is
settled.

## Artwork the page asks for

`src/lib/collage/artPrompt.ts` writes image-generation prompts. Two decisions in
it are about other people's rights rather than about picture quality:

- **Studio and franchise names are removed** from the topic before the prompt is
  written, and replaced with a description of what they actually stand for —
  "Disney" becomes "warm rounded expressive characters with large friendly
  eyes". The agent is told what was swapped so it can pass that on. Naming a
  studio in a prompt is a request to imitate that studio; it is also, usually, a
  worse prompt than the thing the person actually meant.
- **The house style is described, not attributed.** It names materials and a
  palette — cut paper, visible grain, muted mid-century colours — rather than an
  artist or a title. A style is not itself protectable; a request to copy a
  named living illustrator is a different thing, and this deliberately cannot
  produce one.

Neither of these makes the page's *output* free of anybody's rights: what an
image model returns is between the person and whoever they asked. It keeps this
page from being the one doing the asking.

## Services

Background removal and sheet cutting call Needle-operated endpoints
(`remove-background-*.needle.run`, `fastcut.needle.tools`). No model weights are
shipped or run locally, so whatever those models are licensed under is a
question about those services rather than about this repository.

## Brand

Tools' own and are not covered by the MIT grant in `LICENSE`.
